Turn the torso
==============
id: turn_torso
side: both

The shoulders turn from facing the camera to a side view, or from a side view
back toward the camera. The window can run from about 0.45 s to about 1.20 s.

In that window:

  Both shoulders and both hips are seen on most frames.
  Shoulder width is measured as a fraction of the torso length.
  Facing the camera, that fraction starts at 0.48 or more and ends at 0.30
  or less, and it falls by at least 0.20. The turn the other way uses the
  same numbers in reverse.
  The change is already under way at the middle of the window.

A person who keeps facing the camera while walking down the aisle does not count.
It does not depend on another action.
