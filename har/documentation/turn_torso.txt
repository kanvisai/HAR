turn_torso — Turn the torso

The shoulders go from facing the camera to a side view, or back the other way.
The shoulder width, divided by the torso length, has to drop or rise by at
least 0.20, between a front view (0.48 or more) and a side view (0.30 or less).

From this camera, turning toward the shelf is the side view. Category 1 does
this while stopped. The window is about 0.45 to 1.20 seconds.
