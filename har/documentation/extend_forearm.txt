Extend the forearm
==================
id: extend_forearm
side: the arm whose forearm swings out

The forearm swings away from the upper arm. The whole arm does not have to
finish straight. The window can run from about 0.40 s to about 1.15 s, and
overlapping windows of the same arm are merged.

In that window:

  The arm is valid on most frames. A missing point is not used.
  The elbow opens by at least 30 degrees, and it is already opening by the
  middle of the window.
  It starts folded (angle 100 degrees or less) and ends open but not straight
  (angle from 95 to 135 degrees).
  The wrist moves out from the body: at least 0.15 torso lengths farther from
  the shoulder–hip line, or 0.08 farther from the shoulder.
  If the arm finishes straight, that is extend_arm, not this action.

It does not depend on another action.
