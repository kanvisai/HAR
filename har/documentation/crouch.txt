Crouch a little
===============
id: crouch
side: both

The person bends down a little, for example toward a cart. The window can run
from about 0.40 s to about 0.90 s.

In that window:

  Both shoulders and both hips are seen on most frames.
  The vertical distance from the shoulders to the hips shrinks by at least
  18 percent, and by at least 0.012 in normalized image height.
  The whole torso stays about the same size in the image (the scale changes
  by less than about 12 percent). Walking toward the camera makes the body
  larger, and walking away makes it smaller, so those do not count.
  The shortening is already under way at the middle of the window.

It does not depend on another action.
