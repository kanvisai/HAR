Steady wrist-to-waist distance
==============================
id: steady_wrist_waist
side: the wrist that stays put

The distance from the wrist to its hip barely changes, for at least about one
second. This is the hand held still, for example after taking an object and
walking with it.

In that stretch, frame by frame:

  The arm is valid on at least 85 percent of the frames. A missing point is
  not filled in.
  The highest and the lowest wrist-to-hip distance differ by less than 0.06
  torso lengths.
  A frame where the distance starts to change ends the action. Nearby
  stretches are not joined across a long gap.

An arm that is reaching or folding moves more than 0.06 torso lengths and
does not count. It does not depend on another action.
