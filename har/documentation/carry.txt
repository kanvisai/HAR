carry — Carry

While the person is walking, one hand stays at nearly the same distance from
its hip. Over about 1 second that distance varies by less than 0.07 torso
lengths. The hand also sits away from the shoulder–hip line (at least 0.34
torso lengths) or up in front of the body (at least 0.42 of the way from the
hip to the shoulder).

This is category 5: a basket, a bag or a product held while walking. A free
swinging arm fails the steady-distance test.
