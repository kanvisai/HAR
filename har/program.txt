What the program does
=====================

From a skeleton that is already computed, it marks seven gestures for
categories 0, 1 and 5:

  walk          Walk
  stop          Stop
  turn_torso    Turn the torso
  arm_swing     Swing the arm
  carry         Carry
  hand_up       Hand up
  hand_to_body  Hand to the body

The detail of each one is in documentation/, in the txt named with its id.
The libraries are in dependencies.txt. None of the seven gestures depends on another.

Category 0 walks through. The arms swing, and a hand may rise briefly.
Category 1 stops beside the shelf and may turn the torso toward it.
Category 5 keeps walking with at least one hand steady, as if carrying something.

Looking down is not marked. The skeleton has no head point.

Input
-----
poses_full.npy with shape (frames, 8, 2). Coordinates from 0 to 1, the y axis
grows downward. Side is the person's, not the image's. The unit of distance is
the torso. A missing point stays missing.

The file lives at videos/<category>/<clip>/user_<id>/poses_full.npy.
clip.mp4 and meta.json live in the clip folder. fps comes from meta.json.

Programs
--------
detect_json.py writes actions.json next to the npy and temp_output/actions.json.
visualize_ske.py opens the skeleton window and writes temp_output/skeleton.mp4.
visualize_sup.py opens the video window and also writes actions.json,
skeleton.mp4 and overlay.mp4 into temp_output. The list on the right shows
each finished action and how long it lasted.

Space pauses. Arrows, or A and D, move one frame. Q closes.

actions_config.json turns each id on or off. All seven start enabled.
