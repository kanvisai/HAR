Program information
===================

Marks seven gestures from a skeleton that is already computed.
The clips are categories 0, 1 and 5 under /home/ignacio/Escritorio/Company/videos.

  walk          Walk
  stop          Stop
  turn_torso    Turn the torso
  arm_swing     Swing the arm
  carry         Carry
  hand_up       Hand up
  hand_to_body  Hand to the body

Category 0 is a person walking the aisle, with free arms and sometimes a short
spontaneous gesture. Category 1 stops to look and does not take a product.
Category 5 keeps walking while carrying something.

Input
-----
poses_full.npy with shape (frames, 8, 2), one file per person:
  videos/<category>/<clip>/user_<id>/poses_full.npy
Coordinates normalized from 0 to 1, the y axis grows downward.
A point that was not seen is NaN. It is not filled in and it is not drawn.

Points, in this order:
  0 left shoulder
  1 right shoulder
  2 left elbow
  3 right elbow
  4 left wrist
  5 right wrist
  6 left hip
  7 right hip

Side is the person's, not the image's. Distances are measured in torso lengths
(from the shoulder center to the hip center). The x axis is scaled by the
frame width divided by the height, so a horizontal arm is not measured short.

clip.mp4 and meta.json live in the clip folder, next to the user folder.
fps comes from meta.json. The skeleton is smoothed with a 3-frame median.
A missing point stays missing.

What is drawn
-------------
Only points that are present in the npy. No bone is drawn to a missing joint.

Programs
--------
detect_json.py writes actions.json next to the npy and temp_output/actions.json.
visualize_ske.py opens the skeleton window and writes temp_output/skeleton.mp4.
visualize_sup.py opens the video window and also writes actions.json,
skeleton.mp4 and overlay.mp4 into temp_output. The list on the right shows
each action when it ends, with its time range. A row stays "In progress"
while the action is still going.

Space pauses. Arrows, or A and D, move one frame. Q closes.

actions_config.json turns each id on or off. All seven start enabled.
The detail of each action is in documentation/.
The notes on what the three categories look like are in gestures_0_1_5.txt.

Actions
-------
walk
  The hips travel at least 0.45 torso lengths in about 0.9 seconds.

stop
  The hips stay inside a small patch for about 1.1 seconds. Frames that are
  already a walk do not count.

turn_torso
  The shoulders go from facing the camera to a side view, or back.
  The shoulder width has to change by at least 0.20 torso lengths.

arm_swing
  While walking, the wrist-to-hip distance rises and falls by at least 0.16
  torso lengths. That is a free arm.

carry
  While walking, one hand stays at a nearly fixed distance from the hip
  (spread under 0.07) and sits out from the torso or up in front of the body.

hand_up
  A hand rises from below the chest to about shoulder height.
  The face is not measured: there is no head point.

hand_to_body
  A hand is out from the torso for at least 1 second and against the hip for
  at least 2 seconds. That pattern is not called c0, c1 or c5.
