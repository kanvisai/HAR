Program information
===================

Marks six gestures from a skeleton that is already computed.

  extend_arm            Extend the arm
  extend_forearm        Extend the forearm
  retract_arm           Retract the arm
  turn_torso            Turn the torso
  crouch                Crouch a little
  steady_wrist_waist    Steady wrist-to-waist distance

Folder 3 is people putting objects into a cart: reach, fold, turn, and a small crouch.
Folder 4 is people taking something from a shelf: the arm goes out, comes back,
then the wrist stays at a steady distance from the waist while they walk with it.
Folder 0 is people walking past. It is the control set.

Input
-----
poses_full.npy with shape (frames, 8, 2), from 0_npy, 3_npy or 4_npy.
Coordinates normalized from 0 to 1, the y axis grows downward.
A point YOLO did not see is NaN. It is not filled in and it is not drawn.

Points, in this order:
  0 left shoulder
  1 right shoulder
  2 left elbow
  3 right elbow
  4 left wrist
  5 right wrist
  6 left hip
  7 right hip

Side is the person's, not the image's. Distances are measured in torso lengths
(from the shoulder center to the hip center). The x axis is scaled by the
frame width divided by the height, so a horizontal arm is not measured short.

The npy lives in <set>_npy/<clip>/poses_full.npy. clip.mp4 and meta.json live
in <set>/<clip>/. fps comes from meta.json; if it is missing, 12.5 is used.
The skeleton is smoothed with a 3-frame median. A missing point stays missing.

What is drawn
-------------
Only points that are present in the npy. No bone is drawn to a missing joint.

Programs
--------
detect_json.py writes actions.json next to the npy and temp_output/actions.json.
visualize_ske.py opens the skeleton window and writes temp_output/skeleton.mp4.
visualize_sup.py opens the video window and also writes actions.json,
skeleton.mp4 and overlay.mp4 into temp_output. The list on the right shows
each action when it ends, with its time range. A row stays "In progress"
while the action is still going.

Space pauses. Arrows, or A and D, move one frame. Q closes.

actions_config.json turns each id on or off. All six start enabled.
The detail of each action is in documentation/.

Actions
-------
extend_arm
  Extend the arm.
  Over about 0.4 to 1.15 seconds the elbow opens, or a straight arm reaches
  away from the torso. From above, a hand that finishes far from the body
  counts even if the elbow stays under 130 degrees.

extend_forearm
  Extend the forearm.
  The elbow opens and the forearm swings out, but the arm does not finish
  straight. The angle starts at 100 degrees or less and ends between 95 and 135.

retract_arm
  Retract the arm.
  Over about 0.4 to 1.15 seconds the elbow closes, or the hand comes back
  to the body after being out. A reach away from the torso does not count.

turn_torso
  Turn the torso.
  The shoulders go from facing the camera to a side view, or back.
  The shoulder width has to change by at least 0.20 torso lengths.

crouch
  Crouch a little.
  The torso gets shorter in the image while the person stays about the same
  size, so walking toward or away from the camera does not count.

steady_wrist_waist
  Steady wrist-to-waist distance.
  For about a second or more, the wrist stays at nearly the same distance
  from its hip. The spread in that stretch is under 0.06 torso lengths.
  This is the hand held still while the person moves with an object.
