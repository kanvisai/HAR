Program information
===================

Detects gestures of concealing a product from a skeleton that is already computed.
It does not see the object: it marks arm and torso movements consistent with
hiding it after it is already in the hand.

Input
-----
poses_full.npy with shape (frames, 8, 2). Coordinates normalized from 0 to 1,
the y axis grows downward. poses.npy is not used: it is not aligned with the frames.

Points, in this order:
  0 left shoulder
  1 right shoulder
  2 left elbow
  3 right elbow
  4 left wrist
  5 right wrist
  6 left hip
  7 right hip

Side is the person's, not the image's. Distances are measured in torso lengths
(from the shoulder center to the hip center). Height is 0 at the hip and 1 at
the shoulder.

clip.mp4 and meta.json are looked up in the clip folder (the parent of
user_XXXX). fps comes from meta.json; if it is missing, 12.5 is used. The
skeleton is smoothed with a 3-frame median before measuring and drawing. An
elbow that is almost fully closed, or an arm that is too short, is discarded:
it is usually a point misplaced by the tracker, especially from behind.

Turning actions on and off
--------------------------
The file actions_config.json, in the same folder as the programs, lists each
action with "enabled": true or false.

  false  turns it off: it does not appear in the JSON or in the window
  true   turns it back on

If the file is missing, or an id is missing, that action stays on. Save the
file and run again. The filter is applied at the end, so the internal
detectors still run.

Right now cover_with_forearm, turn_torso and crouch are false. The rest are true.

"Keep the hand in the pocket or at the hip" is computed from "Lower the hand
toward the pocket", before the filter. If you turn off only the drop, the stay
at the hip can still appear. To remove both, also turn off hand_at_pocket.

Programs
--------
All three read the npy. If no path is given, they use clip 61
(videos/6/...clip61.../user_15361/poses_full.npy).

detect_json.py
  Writes actions.json in the user folder, next to the npy.
  It also writes temp_output/actions.json. Both are replaced on every run.
  -o changes the JSON next to the npy; the temp_output copy is still written,
  unless it is the same file.
  python3 detect_json.py /path/user_x/poses_full.npy
  python3 detect_json.py /path/user_x/poses_full.npy -o /path/output.json

visualize_ske.py
  Opens a window with the enlarged skeleton (960×720) and the list on the right.
  It also writes temp_output/skeleton.mp4, replaced on every run.
  python3 visualize_ske.py /path/user_x/poses_full.npy

visualize_sup.py
  Opens the mp4 window with the skeleton on top and also writes these three
  files into temp_output, replaced on every run:
    actions.json
    skeleton.mp4
    overlay.mp4
  clip.mp4 is looked up next to the user folder. --video can point at another mp4.
  python3 visualize_sup.py /path/user_x/poses_full.npy
  python3 visualize_sup.py /path/user_x/poses_full.npy --video /path/clip.mp4

temp_output sits next to the programs. The names are fixed: each run overwrites
the previous file. It does not create a new file per clip.

Window
------
Space pauses. Left arrow, or A, steps back one frame. Right arrow, or D, steps
forward one frame. Q, or Escape, closes the window.
At the end it stays on the last frame so the list can be read.

The list on the right starts empty. While an action is still going, the video
frame gets a red border, the arm involved is drawn in red, and "In progress"
appears at the top of the list. When it ends, a row is added with the time
range (start and end), the name and the side. The next action takes the row
below. If they do not fit, the list scrolls to keep the last one visible.
The clock and the frame number sit at the bottom left, so they do not cover
the camera label.

What is in the JSON
-------------------
poses, video, fps, n_frames, duration_s, keypoints, note,
not_observable_with_this_pose, disabled_actions, and the actions list.

Each action has:
  id, name, side (left, right or both)
  frame_start, frame_end
  t_start, t_end, duration_s
  detail: the skeleton measurement that triggered the notice

Actions
-------
bring_to_chest
  Bring the product to the chest or abdomen.
  The wrist drops and moves closer to the abdomen. A straight reach to the shelf does not count.

forearm_on_torso
  Press the forearm against the torso.
  The elbow is bent and the wrist stays near the body axis, at mid height.

hand_to_hand
  Pass the object from one hand to the other.
  Both wrists meet in front of the torso and at least one elbow is bent.
  The body must be seen somewhat from the front; in a pure profile the wrists overlap and this is not used.

cover_with_forearm
  Cover the product with the forearm.
  One wrist crosses in front of the chest midline.
  In actions_config.json this action is currently off.

one_hand_hidden
  One hand in view and the other hidden against the body.
  One wrist stays by the hip and the other is away from the body, at mid height.

lower_to_pocket
  Lower the hand toward the pocket.
  There are two ways in. From above: the hand drops continuously to the hip.
  From the chest: the hand was at mid height or higher, drops to the hip and
  stays there for a moment. In between the tracker may lose the elbow; those
  frames are not required. An arm that was already hanging does not count.

hand_at_pocket
  Keep the hand in the pocket or at the hip.
  Only right after a drop, and only for a short stretch (under a second).
  With these points the pocket cannot be told apart from a hand resting on the hip.

hand_up_torso
  Slide the hand up the torso, against the body.
  The wrist rises from the hip toward the chest without leaving the torso.
  A straight reach up to the shelf does not count.

arms_crossed
  Cross the arms over the chest.
  Each wrist crosses to the opposite side, with the elbows bent.

turn_torso
  Turn the torso sideways to hide the hands.
  The shoulders go from looking wide to looking narrow, the turn is held,
  and one hand stays close to the body.
  A brief change in shoulder width does not count.
  In actions_config.json this action is currently off.

crouch
  Crouch with the hands at leg height.
  The body drops in the image and both wrists end at hip height or below.
  In actions_config.json this action is currently off.

hand_behind_back
  Move one hand to the back or to the opposite hip.
  The wrist is clearly closer to the opposite hip than to its own.
  It is only evaluated when both hips are visible, somewhat from the front.

stiff_arm
  Walk with one arm held against the body, without swinging.
  The body is moving, one arm barely moves relative to its hip and the other does swing.
  The still arm must sit at mid height, not hanging all the way down.

Not visible with this pose
--------------------------
  Open the backpack, bag, or cart.
  Put an arm into a bag and pull it out empty.
  Cover the product with another item.

Those three are noted in the JSON, under not_observable_with_this_pose, and are not emitted as actions.
