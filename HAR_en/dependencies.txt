Dependencies between actions
============================

Almost every gesture is measured from the skeleton alone. They do not read the
result of another gesture. The exception is keeping the hand in the pocket.

Order inside one run
--------------------
1. The skeleton is smoothed and the measurements are computed (height, distances, angle).
2. These gestures are searched, each on its own:
     bring_to_chest
     forearm_on_torso
     hand_to_hand
     cover_with_forearm
     one_hand_hidden
     lower_to_pocket
     hand_up_torso
     arms_crossed
     turn_torso
     crouch
     hand_behind_back
     stiff_arm
3. If the same id and the same side come out in two spans separated by 4 frames
   or fewer, they are merged into one. The detail that is kept is the first span's.
4. From each lower_to_pocket that remains, hand_at_pocket is searched.
5. actions_config.json is applied: anything set to false disappears from the JSON
   and from the window.
6. The list is sorted by the start frame.

The only dependency
-------------------
hand_at_pocket comes from lower_to_pocket.

After the drops are merged, the end frame of each one is inspected. If from
there the wrist stays low and close to its hip, the stay is emitted. Without
a previous drop, the stay does not appear. An arm that was already hanging
produces neither the drop nor the stay.

The configuration filter runs after this step. Turning off only
lower_to_pocket removes the drop from the result, and the stay can still
appear, because it was already created. To remove both, hand_at_pocket must
also be set to false.

Two ways into the same drop
---------------------------
lower_to_pocket is produced by two searches, and both write the same id:

  From above. The hand drops continuously, over about 0.85 s, from above the
  chest down to the hip.

  From the chest. The hand was at mid height or higher, drops to the hip and
  stays there for a moment. In between the elbow may be lost; those frames
  are not required. The look-back reaches about 1 s.

If both describe the same movement and they land within 4 frames, step 3
joins them into a single drop. The stay is computed on that joined drop.

The rest
--------
bring_to_chest, forearm_on_torso, hand_to_hand, cover_with_forearm,
one_hand_hidden, hand_up_torso, arms_crossed, turn_torso, crouch,
hand_behind_back and stiff_arm do not use the result of any other id.

They can overlap in time. The same hand can, for example, drop to the pocket
and, in another stretch, slide up the torso. Each notice is independent.

Actions that look at both arms at once, and come out with side "both":
hand_to_hand, arms_crossed, turn_torso and crouch.
stiff_arm looks at both arms to compare the swing, and the side it stores is
the still arm's.
one_hand_hidden also looks at both, and the side it stores is the hand that
stays by the hip.
