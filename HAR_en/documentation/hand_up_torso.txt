Slide the hand up the torso, against the body
=============================================
id: hand_up_torso
side: the arm that rises

The wrist leaves the hip and rises toward the chest without leaving the torso,
in about 0.55 seconds. This is the gesture of carrying the hand under the
garment, or against the body, upward.

In that window:

  The arm is valid on most frames.
  At the start, height is 0.40 or less.
  It rises at least 0.30, and ends between 0.45 and 0.78.
  Along the stretch, the median distance to the axis is 0.22 torso lengths or less.
  The elbow is not straight: the median angle is 140 degrees or less.

An arm that rises open toward the shelf does not enter: it leaves the axis or
the elbow opens.

It does not depend on another action. It can come after a drop to the pocket;
it does not need that drop to have been detected.
