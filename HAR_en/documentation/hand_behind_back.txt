Move one hand to the back or to the opposite hip
================================================
id: hand_behind_back
side: the arm that crosses toward the other hip

The wrist stays clearly closer to the opposite hip than to its own, for at
least about 0.30 seconds.

In that stretch:

  The arm is valid.
  Both hips are visible, somewhat from the front.
  The distance to the opposite hip is under 0.40 torso lengths, and it is also
  at least 0.15 torso lengths smaller than the distance to its own hip.
  Height is under 0.45.
  The elbow is between 50 and 150 degrees.

In profile the two hips overlap and this measurement is not used.

It does not depend on another action.
