Cover the product with the forearm
==================================
id: cover_with_forearm
side: the arm that crosses

One wrist crosses in front of the chest midline and stays there for at least
about 0.28 seconds.

In that stretch:

  The arm is valid.
  The torso is seen somewhat from the front, so the midline means something.
  The wrist crosses toward the opposite side of the body.
  The elbow is not fully straight (angle under 155 degrees).
  Height is between 0.20 and 0.95.

It does not depend on another action. If both arms cross at the same time,
arms_crossed can also appear: they are different notices.

In actions_config.json this action is currently off.
