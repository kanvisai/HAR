Lower the hand toward the pocket
================================
id: lower_to_pocket
side: the arm that drops

The hand arrives at the hip. There are two ways to see it, and both write this
same id. An arm that was already hanging does not count in either of them.

From above
----------
Over a window of about 0.85 seconds the hand drops continuously:

  The arm is valid on most frames.
  At the start it is high: height 0.75 or more, and the median of the previous
  frames is also high.
  Height keeps falling (the middle frame sits between the start and the end).
  The fall is at least 0.40.
  At the end, height is 0.32 or less and the wrist is 0.48 torso lengths or
  less from its hip.

From the chest
--------------
This covers a shorter drop, or the case where the tracker loses the elbow on
the way. The search looks for the first frame where the hand is already at the hip:

  The arm is valid, height is 0.22 or less, and the distance to the hip is
  0.32 torso lengths or less.
  The previous frame was not yet that low. An arm that was already resting
  down there is not repeated.

From that frame it looks back, between about 0.24 s and about 1.05 s, skipping
frames where the arm is not valid. There must be a valid wrist at height 0.55
or more, a recent median that is also high, and a fall of at least 0.35.

After that, the hand must stay low for about 0.40 s: height under 0.30 and
distance to the hip under 0.42.

The notice runs from the high frame to the frame where it enters the hip. The
time it stays there does not extend this action: that, if it lasts, is
hand_at_pocket.

If both forms describe the same gesture and they land within 4 frames, they
are merged into a single drop.

This action does not read any other id. hand_at_pocket does read this one:
see dependencies.txt.
