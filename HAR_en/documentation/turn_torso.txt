Turn the torso sideways to hide the hands
=========================================
id: turn_torso
side: both

The shoulders go from looking wide to looking narrow, the body stays sideways
and one hand stays close to the torso. The window is about 0.70 seconds.

In that window:

  The torso is valid on most frames.
  At the start, shoulder width is wide (at least 0.48 torso lengths of width
  relative to torso height).
  At the end, that width has fallen below 0.22 and the fall is at least 0.26.
  Just afterwards, the body stays narrow: the median of the following frames
  remains sideways.
  At least one hand stays near the axis during the turn (median distance under
  0.35 torso lengths).

A brief change in shoulder width, without staying sideways, does not count.
Already being in profile the whole time does not count either: the change from
front to side is required.

It does not depend on another action.

In actions_config.json this action is currently off.
