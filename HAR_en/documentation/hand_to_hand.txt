Pass the object from one hand to the other
==========================================
id: hand_to_hand
side: both

Both wrists meet in front of the torso, with at least one elbow bent, for at
least about 0.5 seconds.

In that stretch:

  Both arms are valid.
  The separation between wrists is under 0.28 torso lengths.
  The body is seen somewhat from the front (the shoulders are not almost on the same line).
  The more bent elbow has an angle between 50 and 145 degrees.
  Both hands are at mid height: between 0.14 and 0.62.

In a pure profile the wrists overlap in the image even if each one is in its
own place, and that case is not used.

It does not depend on another action.
