Crouch with the hands at leg height
===================================
id: crouch
side: both

The body drops in the image and both wrists end at hip height or below. The
window is about 0.70 seconds.

In that window:

  The torso is valid on most frames.
  The hip center drops in the image at least a little (0.045 in 0-to-1 coordinates).
  At the end, both wrists are valid and both are below height 0.25.

In actions_config.json this action is currently false: the detector can find
it, and the filter removes it from the JSON and from the window. To see it,
set it to true and run again.

It does not depend on another action.
