Cross the arms over the chest
=============================
id: arms_crossed
side: both

Each wrist crosses to the opposite side of the chest, with the elbows bent,
for at least about 0.30 seconds.

In that stretch:

  Both arms are valid.
  The body is seen somewhat from the front.
  Both wrists cross inward.
  Both elbows have an angle under 155 degrees.
  Both hands are between height 0.30 and 1.05.

If only one arm crosses, the matching notice is cover_with_forearm.
This one requires both.

It does not depend on another action.
