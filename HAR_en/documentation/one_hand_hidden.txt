One hand in view and the other hidden against the body
======================================================
id: one_hand_hidden
side: the hand that stays by the hip

One wrist stays low, by its hip, and the other is away from the torso, at mid
height. It lasts at least about 0.35 seconds.

In that stretch:

  Both arms are valid.
  The hidden hand is under 0.38 torso lengths from its hip and below height 0.35.
  The hand in view is away from the axis (more than 0.55 torso lengths), with
  height between 0.35 and 0.90, and the elbow fairly open (more than 140 degrees).

The stored side is the low hand. If that hand later dropped from the chest,
this can overlap lower_to_pocket or hand_at_pocket; this notice does not use
them to decide.

It does not depend on another action.
