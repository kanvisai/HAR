Keep the hand in the pocket or at the hip
=========================================
id: hand_at_pocket
side: the same side as the drop

The wrist stays by the hip right after it has dropped. With these points the
pocket cannot be told apart from a hand resting on the hip.

It is created only if a lower_to_pocket of that side already exists. It starts
at the end frame of that drop and continues while:

  the arm is valid,
  the distance to its hip is under 0.40 torso lengths,
  height is under 0.32.

The follow stops at 0.70 seconds. If the stretch reaches at least about 0.35
seconds, the stay is emitted. If the hand leaves earlier, there is no notice.

The interval can overlap the end of the drop a little, because it starts on
the same frame where the drop ends.

Turning lower_to_pocket off in actions_config.json does not stop this stay
from being computed: the filter is applied afterwards. To remove it,
hand_at_pocket must also be set to false. The detail is in dependencies.txt.
