Press the forearm against the torso
===================================
id: forearm_on_torso
side: the arm that stays against the body

This is not a trip of the hand. The elbow stays bent and the wrist stays
against that arm's axis, at mid height, for at least about 0.45 seconds.

Frame by frame in that stretch:

  The arm is valid.
  The elbow is bent, with an angle between 55 and 125 degrees.
  The wrist is less than 0.20 torso lengths from the axis (that side's shoulder–hip).
  Height is between 0.18 and 0.62: neither hanging all the way down nor up at the shoulder.

An elbow that is almost fully closed does not count: it is treated as a misplaced point.
A straight arm along the body does not count either: the angle leaves that range.

It does not depend on another action. It can overlap in time with bring_to_chest
or hand_up_torso; each one is recorded on its own.
