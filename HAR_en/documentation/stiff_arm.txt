Walk with one arm held against the body, without swinging
=========================================================
id: stiff_arm
side: the arm that does not swing

The body is moving. One arm barely moves relative to its hip and the other
does swing. The window is about 1 second.

In that window:

  The torso is valid on almost every frame.
  The hip is moving: the person is not standing still.
  Both arms are valid, so they can be compared.
  The still arm varies very little in its position relative to its hip.
  The other arm swings quite a bit more.
  The still arm stays close to its hip (median distance under 0.40 torso lengths)
  and at mid height, between 0.18 and 0.55.

The stored side is the still arm. An arm hanging all the way down, by the
thigh, does not enter: the height falls out the bottom of the range.

It looks at both arms to compare the swing. It does not use the id of any
other action.
