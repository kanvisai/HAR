Bring the product to the chest or abdomen
=========================================
id: bring_to_chest
side: the arm that moves in

The wrist starts away from the body and, in about 0.7 seconds, drops and moves
toward the abdomen. The abdomen is the low point of the torso, closer to the
hip than to the shoulder.

To count, inside that window:

  The arm is valid on most frames.
  At the start, the hand is far from the abdomen (at least 0.60 torso lengths)
  and at mid height or higher (height 0.45 or more).
  At the end, it is close (0.48 torso lengths or less). It has closed at least
  0.22 torso lengths and dropped at least 0.12 in height.
  The end sits between the abdomen and the chest: height between 0.22 and 0.72.

A straight reach to the shelf does not enter: the hand stays high and far
from the abdomen.

It does not depend on another action. If the same arm repeats it with a gap of
4 frames or fewer, the spans are merged into one.
